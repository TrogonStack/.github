# Trogon Stack 🦜

Open-source tools for designing products, coordinating AI agents, and building
event-driven systems.

## TrogonAi

**[TrogonAi](https://github.com/TrogonStack/trogonai) is a distributed agentic
platform for coordinating autonomous AI agents across services and runtimes.**

TrogonAi handles agent lifecycle, inter-agent communication, and durable state.
Clear service boundaries, durable coordination, and observable execution help
teams deploy and scale agents across processes and networks, so they can focus on
agent behavior.

## Atlas

**[Atlas](https://github.com/TrogonStack/atlas) helps teams design products end to
end with AI agents.**

Bring personas, screens, sitemaps, storyboards, and the system behind them into
one shared model, connected through Event Modeling. Give agents clear intent,
review their work, and see how a design change affects the product before it
lands. Keep your team aligned as the design evolves.

## Supporting libraries

- **Event sourcing:**
  [TrogonEventStore](https://github.com/TrogonStack/TrogonEventStore) and its
  [plugins](https://github.com/TrogonStack/TrogonEventStore.Plugins), with
  clients for
  [.NET](https://github.com/TrogonStack/TrogonEventStore-Client-Dotnet) and
  [Rust](https://github.com/TrogonStack/TrogonEventStore-Client-Rust).
- **Schemas and codegen:** [protoc-gen](https://github.com/TrogonStack/protoc-gen).

Written across Rust, Go, C#, TypeScript, and Elixir.
